- The [Center for Astrophysics (CfA) Redshift Survey](https://lweb.cfa.harvard.edu/~dfabricant/huchra/zcat/) is the first systematic measurement of galaxy redshifts, aimed at mapping the large-scale structure of the nearby universe. It was mostly conducted with the 1.5m Tillinghast Telescope at Whipple Observatory, Arizona, and operated by the CfA in Cambridge, Massachusetts, USA.
