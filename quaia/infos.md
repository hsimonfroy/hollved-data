- [Gaia](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Gaia) is a space observatory of the European Space Agency (ESA) that was launched in 2013 and operated until 2025. It has catalogued approximately 1G objects: mostly stars, but also planets, comets, asteroids, and quasars.
- [Quaia](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.17749) is a Quasar catalog constructed from Gaia spectroscopic quasar candidates, processed with unWISE infrared photometry and SDSS spectroscopy. It contains about 1.3M quasars (Bright and Faint) across 73% of the full sky and is the largest-volume spectroscopic quasar catalog to date.
