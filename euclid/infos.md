- [Euclid](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Euclid) is a wide-field space telescope of the European Space Agency (ESA) that was launched in 2023 to be operated until at least 2029, performing visible-light photometry, and near-infrared photometry and spectroscopy. 
- The [first data release (DR1)](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/euclid/dr1-timeline) contains about 1.9M high-probability spectra of Hα Emitters (Hα) over 5% of the full sky, which represents a fraction of the expected 30M galaxy spectra covering 36% of the full sky at the end of the survey.
