- [Euclid](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Euclid) is a wide-field space telescope of the European Space Agency (ESA) that was launched in 2023 to be operated until at least 2029, performing visible-light photometry, and near-infrared photometry and spectroscopy. 
- The [first Quick data release (Q1)](https://www.euclid-ec.org/science/q1/) contains about 30k high-probability galaxy spectra over 0.1% of the full sky, which represents a small fraction of the expected 30M galaxy spectra covering 36% of the full sky at the end of the survey.
