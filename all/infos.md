- Amusingly mirroring the accelerated expansion of the Universe they now endeavor to characterize, **redshift surveys have been growing exponentially**, with the number of catalogued galaxy spectra being **multiplied by almost 10 every 10 years**. 
- This Moore’s law of redshift surveys has been made possible by multiple technological improvements in (i) **detectors** with improved quantum efficiency and lower detector noise from cryogenics, (ii) **optics** with larger telescope apertures and reduced losses along the optical paths, (iii) the **spectroscopic methods** themselves, which allow **massive parallelization** of redshift measurements.
