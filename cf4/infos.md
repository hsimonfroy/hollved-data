- [Cosmicflows-4](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/1538-4357/ac94d8) is a compilation of redshift-independent distance estimations of about 56k individual galaxies gathered into 38k groups up to z~0.1, from observations spanning from the 1990s to the 2020s.
- Providing absolute distance calibration for the entire catalog requires climbing a ladder of methodologies: local geometric distances of stellar parallax, Detached Eclipsing Binaries (DEB), and masers, anchor the Cepheid Period-Luminosity Relation (CPLR) and the Tip of the Red Giant Branch (TRGB), which in turn anchor the Tully-Fisher relation (TF), Fundamental Plane (FP), Type Ia Supernovae (SNIa), Surface Brightness Fluctuations (SBF), and Type II Supernovae (SNII).
