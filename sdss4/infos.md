- The [Sloan Digital Sky Survey (SDSS)](https://www.sdss.org/) is a wide-field imaging and spectroscopic survey conducted with the dedicated 2.5m Sloan Telescope at Apache Point Observatory, New Mexico, USA.
- The [Baryon Oscillation Spectroscopic Survey (BOSS)](https://www.sdss3.org/surveys/boss.php) is the cosmological program of SDSS-III (2009-2014), leading to 1.3M galaxy spectra divided into Low-redshift (LOWZ) and Constant stellar MASS (CMASS).
<!-- Also https://www.sdss4.org/surveys/boss -->
- The [extended Baryon Oscillation Spectroscopic Survey (eBOSS)](https://www.sdss4.org/surveys/eboss/) is the cosmological program of SDSS-IV (2014-2019), extending BOSS to higher redshifts with Luminous Red Galaxies (LRG), Emission Line Galaxies (ELG), and Quasars (QSO).
- Together, BOSS and eBOSS catalogued over 2M galaxies across 24% of the full sky up to redshift z~2.2.
